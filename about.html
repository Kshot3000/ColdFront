About the hub

The All-in-One Bears Fan Hub

THE COLD FRONT is an independent Chicago Bears fan hub — news, injuries, betting odds, game stats & schedule, roster, and practice intel in one place. Built for Midwest football energy (Soldier Field cold, lake wind, orange & blue grit) without the fluff. No accounts, no backend — it runs in your browser and degrades gracefully when the network goes whiteout. Unofficial. Not NFL- or Bears-affiliated.

Why Cold Front? One tab for the whole Bears week — practice whispers on Wednesday, the wire on Friday, kickoff wind on Sunday, film on Monday. Built because the feeds we love live in six different apps, and lake-effect football deserves a home that feels like Chicago: navy, orange, steel, and snow that never quite stops.

Privacy

short version

This is a static fan site — there are no accounts, no tracking profiles, and nothing you type here is sent anywhere except the provider you asked for (paste a data key and it goes only to that API; the rest stays in your browser's local storage on your device). When display ads are enabled, our ad partner (e.g. Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve and measure ads; you can opt out of personalized ads in your Google ad settings. Questions? Optional data sources

BYO-key upgrades · stored only in this browser

Everything above is keyless and works out of the box. For deeper stats, available injury reports, or a fully independent second wire, drop a free key into a box below. It lives in this browser's local storage only — it's never written to the site or shared, and it's sent only to that provider.

Odds API · free tier

Full Vegas board

The odds page already asks for a key right where it's used (subject to your plan). It stays in this browser, same as everything else — no need to set it here.

Enterprise feeds — Sportradar, Opta/Stats Perform, LSports, SportsDataIO — are real-time, low-latency feeds priced for production. They're documented as BYO-key options in the README rather than hard-wired into a static fan site.
TheSportsDB · free tier

A second, independent wire

When a key is set, standings, the roster, and the schedule can read a completely different provider if the league wire is ever unreachable. Different company, different CDN — the site keeps working either way.
