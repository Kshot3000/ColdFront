About the site

Why There's a Weather Site About Football

THE COLD FRONT is an independent fan site built for the kind of football the Midwest does best: the front moves through, the lake dumps its snow, the crowd is in their layers, and nobody asks the referee to check the thermostat. No ads, no accounts, no backend — the whole thing runs in your browser and degrades gracefully when the network blizzards out.

Site facts
  • 🧊 Theme: Midwest winter football — cold fronts, lake effect, crunchers.
  • 📡 Data: ESPN league wire, Polymarket, Open-Meteo weather — all keyless, all in-browser.
  • 🔌 Offline: every feed you've seen once caches to this device for the whiteouts.
  • 🚫 Not affiliated with the NFL, the Chicago Bears, or any bookmaker. Fan-made, stats-checked, weather-obsessed.
