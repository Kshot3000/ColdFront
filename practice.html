Practice & the Facility

The modern NFL keeps practice doors closed — so this page is the honest version: what the week looks like, where it happens, and a tracker you can keep up yourself as the camp and season news drops.

The buildings

uptown & the lakefront

🏢 Halas Hall

The Bears' home base at 65 E. Grand Ave., Chicago — office, locker rooms, film rooms and the place where media availability happens. Named for George Halas, the franchise's founder, "Papa Bear."

🌊 Navy Pier Practice Facility

The practice field on the Chicago lakefront, at Navy Pier (600 E. Grand Ave.). When the wind is out of the northwest, that field turns into a blizzard with goalposts. That's not a metaphor; that's the job description.

🏟️ Soldier Field

Home games at 6161 S. McCormick Blvd. — the open-air coliseum where the cold front does its best work in the fourth quarter. South Lot tailgating is its own sport.

The honest bit: since the league closed open practices in 2011, nobody — fans or media — gets into the building for a session. What you do get is the official media availability schedule, pressers, and the weekly report changes. This page tracks that, and nothing it can't.

Where to follow the week

official
Midwest rule of thumb: in this town, a practice-week headline about the weather is a legitimate injury story. Lake-effect gusts off the lake have ended careers and started fan chants. Dress like you're going to Navy Pier — you're going to read about it.
